INDEPENDENT APPLICATION TESTING SERVICE

Grace App Testing Engagements (GATE)

Helping developers build better applications through practical testing and constructive feedback.

Grace App Testing Engagements (GATE) is an independent, founder-led service managed by Grace Alonge. I work with emerging developers, product builders, student innovators and early-stage teams to review applications, check key user journeys and communicate observations clearly.

Manual application testingUsability evaluationBug documentationStructured feedback

What I can help with

  • Functional checks: checking agreed features, buttons, forms, navigation and key workflows against the expected behaviour supplied for the engagement.
  • Usability evaluation: reviewing whether screens, instructions and user journeys are understandable and practical to follow.
  • Form and input validation: checking required fields, invalid entries, helpful error messages and basic input handling.
  • Cross-device and browser checks: reviewing agreed pages and workflows on the devices and browsers available for the engagement.
  • Persistence and connectivity checks: where relevant, checking whether saved information remains available and how the application behaves during agreed offline or connection changes.
  • Testing documentation: recording steps, expected and actual results, evidence where appropriate, and questions that need the developer's clarification.

Who the service supports

The service is intended for independent developers, emerging product builders, student innovators, hackathon teams and early-stage founders who need a careful outside review of an application or prototype.

How an engagement works

01

Initial enquiry: share a brief description of the application, its current stage and the kind of feedback you need.

02

Project assessment: we discuss the purpose, target users, features to be checked, available platforms, timeline and any constraints.

03

Agreed scope and access: before testing begins, we agree the tasks, test accounts or fictional test data, access method, deliverables and support arrangements.

04

Testing and documentation: I carry out the agreed checks and record results, observations and issues that may need further investigation.

05

Report and close-out: I share the agreed findings, clarify questions and discuss any permitted follow-up or retesting.

Engagement arrangements

Each request is assessed individually. Depending on the project, an engagement may be volunteer-based, operationally supported or sponsored. Any contribution, reimbursement or payment arrangement is discussed and agreed before work starts; no fee or commitment is assumed before the scope is understood.

The scope, timeline, access, test data, communication channel and expected report are agreed in advance. I may decline or defer a request where the requirements, access or support needed are not clear or practical.

Working principles and boundaries

  • Testing is limited to the agreed scope and available devices, browsers, accounts and test data.
  • Findings are documented objectively. An observation is not described as a confirmed defect until the product team has assessed it.
  • Testing feedback is intended to support product improvement; it is not a guarantee that every issue will be found.
  • This service does not provide penetration testing, specialist security audits, formal compliance certification or accredited quality assurance assessments.
  • For health-related or sensitive prototypes, only fictional or expressly authorised test data should be used. Real patient records and other sensitive personal information should not be shared for testing.
  • Confidential information, credentials and internal product materials are handled only as agreed for the engagement and are not published in portfolio materials without permission.
