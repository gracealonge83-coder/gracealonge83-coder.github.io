# QubWatch — Product Development

**Project type:** Product development  
**Programme:** Qubators AI Foundry Cohort 2  
**Status:** In development / refinement

## Overview

QubWatch is a business activity monitoring product concept for inventory-based businesses. It is designed to flag unusual activity for authorised review.

Its guiding principle is: **“Signals for review. Not proof of wrongdoing.”** It is intended to support human review, not make accusations or autonomous decisions.

## Problem being explored

Owners and managers of busy retail businesses may not always be present to review sales, refunds, discounts and stock movements. QubWatch explores ways to make noteworthy activity easier to notice.
