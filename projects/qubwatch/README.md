## Project overview

QubWatch is a business activity monitoring product for inventory-based businesses such as supermarkets, pharmacies and retail shops. It is designed to flag unusual activity so authorised users can review it.

Its guiding principle is: **“Signals for review. Not proof of wrongdoing.”** QubWatch supports human attention and investigation; it does not make accusations or autonomous decisions.

## Problem being explored

Owners and managers cannot always be present to review activity across their business locations. Important sales, refunds, discounts or stock movements may be difficult to spot when they rely on manual updates or scattered reports. QubWatch explores a practical way to bring noteworthy activity into one place for review.

## Product principles

- Designed for authorised business users and human review.
- Highlights activity for attention without treating an alert as proof of misconduct.
- Focuses on inventory-based, high-transaction businesses.
- Uses demo data for portfolio demonstrations.
