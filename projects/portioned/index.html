02 / APPLICATION TESTING & QA · URIBUILD PRIVATE BETA

Portioned

Hands-on testing. Clear observations. Useful feedback.

Portioned is a budgeting application. I joined its private beta to work through assigned test activities, explore key workflows using fictional practice data and document observations for review.

Private beta testingFunctional checksTest observationsWorkflow review

What I tested

  • Profile setup, profile information and opening balances.
  • Overview totals and decimal amount handling.
  • Expense entry and required-field validation.
  • Recurring expenses shared across multiple practice profiles.
  • Portion allocation and the effect of paid records on balances and activity.
  • Borrowing records, repayment schedules and repayment updates.
  • CSV export and currency display.

Examples of observations

01

Recurring expense total: After editing a recurring expense amount from ₦10,000 to ₦12,000, the Overview Total Expenses figure remained at ₦81,000 during that check. I recorded the result for review.

03

Practice profile management: I noticed that an incorrectly created practice profile did not have an available delete option.

These are observations from my testing sessions. They are not presented as defects confirmed by the product team.

My testing approach

I follow the assigned scenario, enter fictional practice data, check the resulting screen or records, compare what happened with the expected behaviour and record the outcome. I distinguish between a passed check, an observation and something that needs clarification.

What I am learning

This experience is helping me practise structured testing, careful observation, checking related screens and records, and communicating results in a way that can support product review.
