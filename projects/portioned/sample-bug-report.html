Issue summary

Report ID
GAE-SAMPLE-001
Title
Overview expense total does not appear to refresh after editing a recurring expense
Area
Recurring expenses / Overview
Environment
Private beta application, tested through the PWA
Test data
Fictional practice data
Priority
Not assigned — for product team assessment
Status
Observation recorded; confirmation by product team not known

Precondition

A practice profile contains a recurring expense and the Overview displays an expense total.

Steps to reproduce

  1. Open the practice profile containing the recurring expense.
  2. Note the current recurring expense amount and the Overview Total Expenses figure.
  3. Edit the recurring expense amount from ₦10,000 to ₦12,000.
  4. Save the change.
  5. Return to the Overview and check the Total Expenses figure.

Expected behaviour

After the edited recurring expense is saved, the relevant Overview expense total should recalculate to reflect the updated amount, according to the application's expense calculation rules.

Actual behaviour observed

During the test, the Overview Total Expenses figure remained at ₦81,000 after the recurring expense amount was changed from ₦10,000 to ₦12,000.

Evidence and notes

The observation was recorded in the tester's notes. No screenshot or private beta data is reproduced in this public sample. The product team's confirmation and the underlying calculation rules were not available to the tester at the time of reporting.

Suggested follow-up

  • Check whether the edited recurring expense is included in the Overview calculation.
  • Confirm whether the Overview is expected to refresh immediately or after another action.
  • Retest the same workflow after any product update and record the result.

This is an anonymised portfolio sample derived from a real tester observation. It is not a claim that the product team confirmed or resolved the issue. Amounts and details are included only to explain the observed test scenario.
